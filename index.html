Paper Bloom

Turn a photo into a layered paper pop-up card.

Filming your card…This takes about 10 seconds
Cutting paper layers…
Tap the card to open it · drag to turn it

1. Add photo

Choose a photo you love. We'll cut it into paper pieces that pop up when the card opens. Landscapes, portraits, pets and flowers all work well.

2. Pop-up style

Choose how the card pops up. We've marked the best fit for your photo as Auto. Tap another style to try it, then open the card to see it.

3. Cover colour Soft pastel

Pick a colour for the front of the card. We've matched one to your photo. Use the rainbow to choose any colour.

4. Cover text optional

Type a message for the front of the card. Select words to style just those, or leave nothing selected to style it all.

5. Send your card

We'll make a short video of your card opening, with a link so the person you send it to can make their own.

Your card is ready to send

Save the video, then send it on WhatsApp, Instagram, email or any app. Copy the message to add the Paper Bloom link.
